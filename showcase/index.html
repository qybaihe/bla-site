CASE STUDY 01 · ELECTRON ASAR & IPC

Case Study 01: Blinding Notion's Electron Clipboard Bridge

Inside morluto/rea's electronStaticAnalysisIpc.ts, REA relies on a strict invariant: "Only a unique exact literal IPC channel match supports an inferred pairing." BLA breaks both halves of this invariant: first by splitting real IPC channel names into ≥9 opaque arithmetic chunks (512 > 256 primitive candidate limit), and second by registering duplicate decoy handlers on the same literal string.
