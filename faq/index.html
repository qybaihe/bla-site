Everything About BLA (`bla-agents`)

Why is BLA Blue while REA is Red?
In cybersecurity, Red Team tools attack and Blue Team tools defend. REA (re = Red) is the offensive reverse-engineering MCP; BLA (bl = Blue / Blind LLM Agents) is the counter-offensive Blue Shield built to exhaust REA and mislead attacking models.
Does BLA slow down my production application at runtime?
No. All opaque predicates use O(1) bitwise number-theoretic invariants (such as (Math.imul(x, x + 1) & 1) === 0), and all Tarjan SCC tarpit meshes and 22-family obligation floods live inside dead branches that never execute on the hot path.
How do I trigger full defense hardening in one click after agent setup?
Run npx bla-agents@latest defend ./dist --manifest from your terminal, or ask your connected coding agent to call the bla_defend MCP tool. It automatically runs release exposure scanning, pre-flight auditing, bankrupt shield injection, and invariant verification.
Does BLA preserve existing GGUF model tensors and Native Binary signatures?
Yes. protectGgufBuffer parses and preserves all original GGUF v2/v3 KV entries and trailing tensor slices while appending BLA enclave metadata, and Android DEX protection recomputes both the 20-byte SHA-1 signature and Adler32 checksum.
